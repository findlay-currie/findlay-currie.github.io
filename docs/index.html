Personal statement

I am an Assurance Engineer at Anturas Consulting Ltd, working on rail, energy and infrastructure projects. My work covers requirements management, verification and validation, hazard management, interface and configuration management, and CDM 2015 compliance, building the evidence clients need to show that what is designed and delivered meets its requirements and is safe.

My foundation is a BEng (Hons) in Mechanical Engineering from Heriot-Watt University and nearly two years delivering capital projects at Seafield, Scotland's largest wastewater treatment works. There I managed civils remediation, worked with contractors, manufacturers and designers on a live operational site, and built the site's digital tools. Earlier management roles in hospitality taught me to lead teams and control costs under pressure.

I am committed to the highest standards of honesty and integrity, and I bring ingenuity and creative thinking to technical challenges.

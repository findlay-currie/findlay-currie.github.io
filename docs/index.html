About Me

I'm an Assurance Engineer at Anturas Consulting Ltd, a boutique infrastructure consultancy based in Edinburgh's New Town. I joined the team in 2026 following a graduate engineering rotation at Veolia Water.

I bring an engineering-first mindset, comfortable working across mechanical, civil, and process disciplines to take problems from investigation through to delivered solutions.
